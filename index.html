    [DOCS]

    NAME

    pomotimer — r/unixporn + 8-bit RPG HUD Pomodoro timer

    KEYBINDINGS

    SpaceStart / Pause current timer
    RReset current timer phase
    NSkip to next phase (Focus / Break)
    /Focus $ task add input
    1 - 3Switch mode (Pomodoro / Short / Long)

    POMODORO ENGINE

    Alternate deep focus blocks (25m default) with short breaks (5m) and long breaks (15m). Adjust durations inline or in dotfiles.conf with 8-bit chiptune alerts.

    QUEST LOG (TO-DO LIST)

    Manage tasks with tags, pomodoro estimates, and Active Quest targeting. Data is saved automatically in your browser (localStorage + cookies).

    LICENSE & PRICING

    FREE FOREVER (GPL / FOSS Spirit): 100% free, no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and zero distractions. Pure Arch Linux productivity.

    POMODORO TECHNIQUE

    Created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, it breaks work into 25-minute intervals separated by short breaks to maintain focus, avoid burnout, and beat procrastination. Take a long break every 4 cycles.
